# Research index

## PC recompilation and renderer

- Source-generation dependency map: the private staging report `16_GENERATION_DEPENDENCY_MAP.md` describes missing ROM-specific files, tool versions and generator behavior. Public source candidate excludes those ROM-specific inputs.
- Runtime and renderer experiments: separate from game-derived generated guest code and RSP output, which are not included here.
- Track seam and widescreen menu reports: known issues are summarized in the PC candidate; no renderer correction is claimed.

## USA / Exotica

- Track and vehicle extraction/research exists in the private project.
- USA donor-backed roster experiments and a partial 196-face La Bomba mesh are research only.
- Exotica donor prototypes and renderer experiments remain incomplete.
- Vehicle transforms/attachment to a moving donor were not established; no complete imported car was shown in gameplay.
- Do not copy extracted geometry, textures, audio, ROMs, or captures into this hub.

## Platforms

- [3DS ARM11 handoff](https://github.com/OWNER/cruisn-world-3ds) and [separate 3DS RT64 handoff](https://github.com/OWNER/cruisn-world-3ds-rt64) are distinct incomplete projects.
- [Vita handoff](https://github.com/OWNER/cruisn-world-vita) records early device log evidence only.

## Velocity and launcher

Velocity is a separate GBA recompilation effort; interactive gameplay is not validated. Its source/build depends on GBARecomp under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0. This hub contains no source code, replay inputs, ROM, BIOS, generated output, or binary from Velocity. The launcher is a companion project; it does not establish Velocity playability.

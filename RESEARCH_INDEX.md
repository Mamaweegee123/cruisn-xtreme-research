# Research index

## PC recompilation and renderer

- [PC development handoff](CRUISN_WORLD_PC_HANDOFF.md) summarizes the architecture, November 5 local gameplay check, current limitations, and source-generation blockers.
- The private generation dependency map records missing ROM-specific files, tool versions and generator behavior. It is not part of this public hub; the public PC source candidate excludes those ROM-specific inputs.
- Runtime/renderer integration is incomplete in the public source candidate and separate from generated guest and RSP code, which are not included.
- Track seam and widescreen-menu issues remain unresolved; no renderer correction is claimed.

## USA / Exotica

- USA donor-backed roster experiments and a partial 196-face La Bomba mesh are research only.
- Exotica donor prototypes and renderer experiments remain incomplete.
- Vehicle transforms/attachment to a moving donor were not established; no complete imported car was shown in gameplay.
- Do not copy extracted geometry, textures, audio, ROMs, or captures into this hub.

## Velocity and launcher

[Velocity status](DEVELOPMENT_STATUS.md) remains experimental: interactive gameplay is not validated. Its source/build depends on GBARecomp under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0. This hub contains no source code, replay inputs, ROM, BIOS, generated output, or binary from Velocity. The [launcher handoff](https://github.com/OWNER/cruisn-xtreme-launcher) is a companion project; it does not establish Velocity playability.
